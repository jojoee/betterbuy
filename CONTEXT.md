# Betterbuy glossary

## Comparison

A calculation that evaluates two manually entered offers by cost per comparable
size.

## Draft Comparison

The editable offer values currently shown in the calculator. A draft is not
saved until the user explicitly saves it.

## Saved Comparison

A locally stored snapshot of a valid comparison, including its input and
calculated result.

## Pinned Comparison

A saved comparison deliberately kept at the start of history. At most five
saved comparisons may be pinned.

## History

The user-controlled local collection of saved comparisons. History is capped
at 50 entries and never leaves the browser.
